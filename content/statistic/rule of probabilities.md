# A) Rule of Probabilities ?

* chain rule
* sum rule: $p(X)=\sum_{Y}p(X,Y)$
* product rule: $p(X,Y)=p(Y\mid X)p(X)$
* relation with [[Bayes theorem]]

$$
\displaystyle p(Y\mid X)=\frac{p(X\mid Y)p(Y)}{p(X)}
$$

Evidence 는 다음과 같이 sum 과 product rule 을 동시에 사용하여 계산한다.

$$
p(X)=\sum_{Y}p(X\mid Y)p(Y)
$$
