# A) Mean ?

If we have a data set consisting of the values $a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{n}$, then the arithmetic mean $A$ is defined by the formula:  

$$
 A=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_{i}=\frac{a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}}{n}
$$
