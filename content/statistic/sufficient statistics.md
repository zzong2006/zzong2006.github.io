# 1. Sufficient Statistics ?

* Sufficiency : $T(X_1, X_2,…, X_n)$ is said to have ‘sufficiency’ for the parameter, if the conditional of $X_1, X_2,… X_n$ given $T=t$ does not rely on the value of the parameter. And the $T(X_1,X_2,X_3,…,Xn)$ is called ‘Sufficient Statistics.’
