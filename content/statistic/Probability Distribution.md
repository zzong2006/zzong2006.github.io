# Probability Distribution ?

* Discrete 확률 분포
	* we use discrete probability distributions to model categorical variables.
		* i.e., variables that take a finite set of unordered values.
	* Discrete distributions are also often used to construct probabilistic models that combine a finite number of continuous distributions.
