# Product Rule ?

$$
p(\boldsymbol{x},\boldsymbol{y})=p(\boldsymbol{y}\mid\boldsymbol{x})p(\boldsymbol{x})=p(\boldsymbol{x}\mid\boldsymbol{y})p(\boldsymbol{y})
$$

* $p(\boldsymbol{x})$: [[marginal distribution]] of the first random variable
* $p(\boldsymbol{y}\mid\boldsymbol{x})$: conditional distribution of second random variable given first

## 해석

* The product rule can be interpreted as the fact that every joint distribution of two random variables can be factorized (or product) of two other distributions.
