# 1. Calibrated Recommendations ?

* Reference
	* paper: https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3240323.3240372
* cailbration 의 목적
	* In the recommended list of items, **calibration** ensures that the various (past) areas of interest of a user are reflected with their corresponding proportions.
	* When a user has watched, say, 70 romance movies and 30 action movies, then it is reasonable to expect the personalized list of recommended movies to be comprised of about 70% romance and 30% action movies as well.
* 이 논문에서 제시하는 것
	* metrics for quantifying the degree of calibration
	* a simple yet effective re-ranking algorithm for post-processing the output of recommender systems
