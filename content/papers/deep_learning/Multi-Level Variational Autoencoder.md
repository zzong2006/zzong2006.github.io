The [[machine_learning/deep_learning/Variational Autoencoder|VAE]] model assumes that the observations are independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.).

- In the case of grouped observations, this assumption is no longer true.
- The VAE model takes no advantage of the knowledge of the grouping.
