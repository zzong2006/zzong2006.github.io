# A) Inscrutable ?

Impossible to understand or interpret.

# B) Example

> I have seen companies use low-code tools for processes where precise analytics of the underlying data is critical. But then, when viewing the underlying data model, come to find that it’s **inscrutable**: what does `user_attribute_47` mean? I moved a field from page 1 to page 2 of the application and now the data are in separate fields?

Reference: [Why I'm skeptical of low-code - Nick Scialli | Senior Software Engineer](https://nick.scialli.me/blog/why-im-skeptical-of-low-code/)

# C) 관련어

* unfathomable (헤아릴 수 없는)
* enigmatic (수수께끼 같은)
* opaque (불투명한, 알기 힘든)
* incomprehensible (이해할 수 없는)
