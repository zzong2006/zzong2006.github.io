# Triangular Matrix ?

$$
L=\left[\begin{array}{ccccc}\ell_{1,1} & & & & 0 \\ \ell_{2,1} & \ell_{2,2} & & & \\ \ell_{3,1} & \ell_{3,2} & \ddots & & \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & \\ \ell_{n, 1} & \ell_{n, 2} & \ldots & \ell_{n, n-1} & \ell_{n, n}\end{array}\right]
$$
